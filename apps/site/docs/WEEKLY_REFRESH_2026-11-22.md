# CFB Apex November 22 release evidence

The native site refresh publishes every Week 12 result as a verified final, brings in the full Week 13 slate, publishes the November 22 AP and AFCA Coaches editions, and advances the GameDay board weeks to 6–13. It was due Sunday, November 22 and was captured Monday, November 23, after the Week 13 cutover (14:00 UTC on November 22) had already passed, so the default board shows Week 13 as soon as this release is live. It does not claim that every independently dated editorial or statistical module is newly complete. The owner's standing approval (renewed November 23) covers this branch, its pull request, the merge after green checks and the matching native production deployment. No approving review is submitted for the owner.

## Finals

Every Week 12 final satisfies the finals rule: the ESPN event is completed with state `post`, each side's period scores (including overtime) sum to its final, and the NCAA FBS scoreboard lists the same contest as `F` with the same score for the same home and away sides. All 66 board finals and the four FCS-opponent finals are final on both boards at the cutoff, so no result rests on a school page and none is promoted because its kickoff time passed.

Game ids never change. Each final keeps the id its scheduled row published on November 16. One final was played at a different listed instant than the published kickoff: Oklahoma State at Arizona State (published 03:15 UTC November 22, played 03:30 UTC, same Eastern date November 21). It keeps the published kickoff as `originally_scheduled_kickoff_utc`.

The four FCS-opponent games (Chattanooga at Alabama, Wofford at Ole Miss, Tennessee Tech at Mississippi State, Samford at Auburn) are verified finals that count in the whole-team records and stay outside the interface.

Pregame AP ranks on the Week 12 finals are the November 16 edition, the poll that preceded those games. The November 15 release never carried that edition (the provider published it after that capture); it is archived in `poll_history` with its own date.

The Week 12 finals story (`week12-verified-finals`) is generated by the builder from the verified finals and those pregame ranks only: the count of AP-ranked teams that played and lost and each ranked loss with its score. It contains no opinion copy.

## Polls

Each poll keeps its own release date.

- AP Top 25: the November 22 edition, published by ESPN and matched on the NCAA table. The November 8 edition the site had carried and the November 16 edition are archived in `poll_history` with their own dates.
- AFCA Coaches: the November 22 edition, equal row for row on ESPN and the USA Today coaches table. The November 15 edition is archived.
- Composite: both tables are editions of one date again, so the editorial composite is the November 22 average.
- Movement: AP movement notes compare November 22 with November 16. Kentucky (17) and Memphis (24) left the Top 25; Oklahoma and Missouri entered.

ESPN's default rankings document also carries a third poll dated November 23 (provider poll id 20). The site does not publish it and the builder does not read its rows. The CFP committee rankings are not part of this release; they follow their own release on Tuesday, November 24.

## Week 13 slate

The slate is built from ESPN scoreboards (groups=80, one call per date, November 24–28) and matched to the NCAA Week 13 inventory contest by contest: 69 of 69. No game has an FCS opponent.

All 69 kickoffs and networks are announced, and every kickoff is the same instant on ESPN and the NCAA scoreboard. No kickoff is settled by a school page and none is held.

A first capture the same morning (06:47 PT) was different: the NCAA scoreboard had not yet filled in its Saturday times, one contest (Texas State at Utah State) was not listed there, and sixteen kickoffs would have rested on host-school schedules with one held as TBA. That capture was never published. By the 10:35 PT capture the NCAA scoreboard listed all 69 contests with start times equal to the provider's, so `scripts/week13-host-kickoffs.mjs` carries no rule. One observation from that first capture is worth keeping: for Duke at Wake Forest (Saturday, November 28) ESPN and the NCAA scoreboard both list 3:30 PM ET, while godeacs.com showed 3:00 p.m. on the morning of November 23. The published kickoff follows the two boards that agree.

If the boards stop agreeing, the builder stops with the unsettled list and the gate fails; a reviewed host-school rule in `week13-host-kickoffs.mjs` is then the only way to publish that kickoff. The builder ignores a reviewed rule once the NCAA scoreboard states the provider instant itself.

The Week 13 marquee is USC at UCLA, chosen by the board's own rule (`lib/homepage.ts` `featuredGame`: lowest combined AP rank, earlier date first) on the November 22 AP edition. The two AP-ranked pairings are USC at UCLA and Oklahoma at Missouri.

## Records

Each team's row on the official NCAA standings page is resolved by the exact NCAA school name. All 138 NCAA rows equal the provider record at this capture; no row is recorded as lagging.

`verify-production-target.mjs` stays on the reviewed version `961d3545-84d0-4c69-8177-7e2387d5eec1` (advanced by PR 70 before this release, read from provider deployment history on November 23 as the newest deployment at 100% traffic).
