# CFB Apex November 15 release evidence

The native site refresh publishes every Week 11 result as a verified final, brings in the full Week 12 slate, and advances the GameDay board weeks to 6–12. It was due Sunday, November 15 and was captured Monday, November 16, after the Week 12 cutover (14:00 UTC on November 15) had already passed, so the default board shows Week 12 as soon as this release is live. It does not claim that every independently dated editorial or statistical module is newly complete. The owner's standing approval of November 15, renewed November 16, covers this branch, its pull request, the merge after green checks and the matching native production deployment. No approving review is submitted for the owner.

## Finals

Every Week 11 final satisfies the finals rule: the ESPN event is completed with state `post`, each side's quarter scores sum to its final, and the NCAA FBS scoreboard lists the same contest as `F` with the same score for the same home and away sides. All 67 are final on both boards at the cutoff, so no result rests on a school page alone and none is promoted because its kickoff time passed.

Game ids never change. Each final keeps the id its scheduled row published on November 8/10. Ten finals were played at a different listed instant than the published kickoff; each keeps the published kickoff as `originally_scheduled_kickoff_utc` and both boards' listed instants as separate observations.

One of them changed its playing date. Ole Miss at Oklahoma was listed for Saturday, November 14 (21:15 UTC) and was played Sunday, November 15 (18:00 UTC): Oklahoma 24, Ole Miss 17. It keeps the id `2026-11-14-ole-miss-at-oklahoma`, carries the Eastern playing date `2026-11-15`, and has an extra host-school receipt (soonersports.com: "Football versus Ole Miss on November 15, 2026, Win, 24, to, 17") that the source gate re-asserts literally.

Pregame AP ranks on the Week 11 finals are the November 8 edition, the poll that preceded those games.

The Week 11 finals story (`week11-verified-finals`) is generated by the builder from the verified finals and those pregame ranks only: the count of AP-ranked teams that played and lost, each ranked loss with its score, and the Sunday game. It contains no opinion copy.

## Polls

Each poll keeps its own release date.

- AFCA Coaches: the November 15 edition, equal row for row (rank, points, record) on ESPN and the USA Today coaches table. The November 8 Coaches edition is archived in `poll_history`.
- AP Top 25: no edition newer than November 8 is published. ESPN's current rankings document carries only the Coaches poll for Week 12; ESPN's Week 11 document and the NCAA AP table both show the November 8 edition. That edition is kept, byte for byte, with its November 8 date. It is not relabeled. The gate fails if a newer AP edition appears, so the next release has to reconcile it.
- Composite: the editorial composite averages two editions of one date. While the AP table is the November 8 edition, the composite stays the published November 8 average rather than mixing a November 8 AP poll with a November 15 Coaches poll. The AP movement notes also keep their November 8 date.

## Week 12 slate

The slate is built from ESPN scoreboards (groups=80, one call per date, November 17–21) and matched to the NCAA Week 12 inventory contest by contest. Four games have an FCS opponent (Wofford at Ole Miss, Tennessee Tech at Mississippi State, Chattanooga at Alabama, Samford at Auburn); each is excluded with ESPN's own conference id for the FCS side as evidence.

All 66 kickoffs and networks are announced. Three kickoffs differ between ESPN and the NCAA scoreboard. Each is settled by the host school's own official schedule, which agrees with ESPN; the NCAA listing stays recorded as a separate observation:

|---|---|---|---|
| Eastern Michigan at Western Michigan, Tue Nov 17 | wmubroncos.com: "Nov 17 (Tue) / 6:00 PM", page instant 23:00 UTC | 23:00 UTC | 00:00 UTC |
| Central Michigan at Buffalo, Wed Nov 18 | ubbulls.com: "Wed, Nov 18, 7:30 pm" | 00:30 UTC (Nov 19) | 00:00 UTC |
| UTEP at Air Force, Fri Nov 20 | goairforcefalcons.com: "Nov 20 / 6:00 PM MT" | 01:00 UTC (Nov 21) | 13:00 UTC (Nov 20) |

Four hosts were added to `official-school-hosts.json` after confirming each is the school's official athletics site: `wmubroncos.com` (linked from wmich.edu), `goairforcefalcons.com` (linked from usafa.edu), `soonersports.com` (linked from ou.edu) and `ubbulls.com` (linked from the Mid-American Conference's football page; buffalo.edu answered 403 to the retrieval and is recorded as not loaded).

The Week 12 marquee is Georgia at South Carolina, chosen by the board's own rule (`lib/homepage.ts` `featuredGame`: lowest combined AP rank, earlier date first) on the AP edition the site publishes.

Each team's row on the official NCAA standings page is resolved by the exact NCAA school name the November 8 release reviewed, never by record or name similarity.

## Engineering and release gates

Run `npm run verify`, `npm run cf:types`, `npm run deploy:dry-run`, `npm run gameday:source-gate`, production dependency audit and generated parity. The source gate (`scripts/check-nov15-sources.mjs`) re-fetches every receipt, requires every saved receipt and live HTTP Date within two hours, checks Age separately, and asserts: the 67/70 week inventories, every final main already publishes unchanged, every Week 11 final's identity, score, quarter sum and kickoff observations against ESPN and NCAA, the Sunday final's school receipt, every Week 12 row against the live listing, the three host-school settlements, the four FCS exclusions, all 138 records, both poll tables, leader totals, and the 752-final full-season audit through `verifyFullSeason`. The builder and the gate retry a non-200 answer twice, a few seconds apart, because some official sites answer an occasional bot-filter 403; only a 200 with a fresh Date is accepted.

`verify-production-target.mjs` advances deliberately to the reviewed version `57cd109c-5604-4d04-9d2b-7dbccc093b28` (PR 63 main `29b1caa` deployment `8650d9bd-aa9d-4adf-b245-c19f7ba7d67d`, 2026-11-10T13:54:47Z), read from provider deployment history on November 16 as the newest deployment at 100% traffic. A newer or split deployment fails closed before this release writes. Rollback is scoped to the same account and Worker and requires an explicit owner decision if it would revert newer unrelated work.

`smoke-gameday.mjs` covers weeks 10 (62 finals), 11 (67 finals) and 12 (66 scheduled).
